Help

Frequently asked questions

What is Carryoo Enterprise?
Carryoo Enterprise is our business logistics offering for centralised control of on-demand goods transportation. Companies can book trucks, tempos, EVs, and two-wheelers for intracity movement, manage users from one account, and track trips with clearer operational visibility.
How does Carryoo help businesses with logistics?
We provide on-demand vehicles for bulk and small consignments, prepaid wallet payments, multi-user access, and trip-level reporting so your team can move goods without cash reimbursements or fragmented vendor coordination.
How long does it take to activate my Enterprise account?
In most cases, activation takes about one business day after we receive your business details and complete basic verification.
Can I get assistance while booking vehicles?
Yes. Enterprise users get onboarding guidance for the booking flow. For booking help, contact support at support@nashikflow.app or write to business@nashikflow.app for account-level questions.
Do you provide a dashboard to track account activity?
Yes. You get access to a web-based dashboard to check wallet balance, trip details, payment options, and user information, along with periodic account statements where enabled.
