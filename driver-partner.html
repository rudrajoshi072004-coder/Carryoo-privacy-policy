Help

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a driver partner job at Carryoo?
Becoming a Carryoo partner is simple:
  1. Download the Carryoo Partner app and register (demo until Play Store launch).
  2. Submit your documents for verification.
  3. Complete onboarding / a practice booking if required.
After verification, you can start receiving trips.
Is there any registration fee to join Carryoo?
No. There is no registration fee to join as a Carryoo Driver Partner. Download the app, register your documents, and complete verification.
Which vehicles can I attach?
You can attach two-wheelers (bike/scooter) and commercial vehicles such as three-wheelers, pickups, and mini trucks for on-demand goods transport — subject to city availability and document checks.
How much can I earn?
Earnings depend on the time you stay online, trip volume, vehicle type, and incentives. Longer engagement usually means more trips and higher weekly earnings.
Can I work part-time?
Yes. You can work part-time or full-time and choose when to go online.
How can I check earnings and payouts?
The partner app shows daily earnings and payout details so you can track what you made and when settlements are due.
Can I refer friends to join Carryoo?
Yes. Referral bonuses may be offered for successful onboarding of eligible vehicle types. Details will be shown in the partner app when the program is active.
I have a query. How do I contact Carryoo?
Email support@nashikflow.app for partner support, or business@nashikflow.app for fleet-owner enquiries.
