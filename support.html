FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Filter by topic or use the search above. Results update instantly.

How do I track my booking?
Open the Carryoo app, go to your active or past orders, and select the trip to see live status. For help, email support@nashikflow.app with your registered mobile number and booking ID.
How do cancellations and refunds work?
Cancellation rules depend on trip status and timing. If a refund applies, it is processed to your original payment method. Contact support@nashikflow.app if a refund is delayed.
Which payment methods are supported?
Carryoo supports common digital payment options in-app (UPI, cards, wallets where enabled). Enterprise accounts can also use prepaid business wallets.
How do I join as a driver partner?
Visit the Driver Partner page, download the partner app (demo until Play Store launch), register your documents, and complete verification. No registration fee.
When do driver partners get paid?
Payouts follow the schedule shown in the partner app. You can check daily earnings and weekly settlements in-app. For payout issues, email support@nashikflow.app.
How do I delete my Carryoo account?
Follow the steps in our Account deletion section, or email support@nashikflow.app with subject “Carryoo — Account deletion request” from your registered contact.
How do I set up a Carryoo Enterprise account?
See Carryoo Enterprise or email business@nashikflow.app. Activation usually takes about one business day after verification.
Do enterprise accounts get a dashboard?
Yes. Enterprise users get a web dashboard for wallet balance, trip details, users, and statements where enabled.
I can’t log in / OTP not received
Check network coverage, wait a minute, and retry. Make sure you use the registered mobile number. If OTP still fails, email support@nashikflow.app with your number and device details.
